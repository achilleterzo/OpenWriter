# ProjectsHome.jsx

Source: [`src/components/ProjectsHome.jsx`](../../../../../src/components/ProjectsHome.jsx)

The Home of the projects, shown in the central area while the side tab **Projects** is active (the documents and the assistant stay mounted, only hidden). It loads the graph (`getProjectGraph`) and draws [ProjectGraph.jsx](ProjectGraph.jsx.md); on top there is a search field that works like the agent's search: the same hybrid search (words and meaning) over every project that shares its knowledge and has an index, whichever project is open (`searchProjects`, main process). The search starts by itself shortly after the last key and a late answer is discarded. The results appear in an overlay panel grouped by project (icon, name, passages with file, lines, headings and the matched words highlighted); the projects with results light up in the network. A click on a project opens it (and switches to its files), a click on a passage also opens the document. A line at the bottom says how the lines were drawn (by meaning, approximate without the model, or not yet).

Only the network area has fixed colours: a soft dark grey gradient and the dark palette (and the chosen accent in its dark form), whatever the theme of the app, through the `.home-dark` class of `styles.css`. The search field and the results panel follow the theme of the app.

The results panel is anchored to the right with the same top and bottom margin, and its left edge is a handle ([PanelResizer.jsx](PanelResizer.jsx.md)) to widen it towards the left (arrows, double click to reset); the width is remembered between sessions (`loadResultsWidth` / `saveResultsWidth` in [layout.js](../../lib/layout.js.md)) and never exceeds the space of the Home.

Each passage of the results knows why it is there: a passage found only by meaning carries a "By meaning" tag (it does not contain the words), and when no passage contains all the words a note says that these contain only some of them. A question with no relevant answer shows "Nothing relevant found" instead of unrelated passages (see the relevance rules in [knowledge-index.cjs](../../../electron/knowledge-index.cjs.md)).

Next to the file of each passage there is the **relevance factor**: a small bar and a percentage, with the bar colored like a traffic light (red under 33%, yellow from 33% to 66%, green above 66%, on the percentage shown) (tooltip: how much the passage stands out from the others for this search, with its `z` score). It is the greater of the closeness in meaning and the closeness by words, and it is missing when the search runs without the meaning model and no word matched.

**Synonyms with AI.** A star button in the search field (on by default, remembered) adds the synonyms of the search. The plain search runs first and answers at once; then the app asks a language model for synonyms: the **research model** (settings → research assistant) when it is turned on and has a model, even if the assistant is off, otherwise the model of the assistant and related terms ([queryExpansion.js](../../lib/queryExpansion.js.md): the model sees only the searched words, with a 15 second limit, and the answer is cached for the session), and searches again with all of them: each term is searched by words and by meaning, and the results are merged ([search-merge.cjs](../../../electron/search-merge.cjs.md)). The terms appear as chips ("Also searched") and a passage found through a synonym says "via <term>". If the AI assistant is off, or the request fails, the search stays the plain one and a line says why.

**Show less relevant too.** At the end of the results (and in the "No relevant results" message) a text button shows also the passages the relevance filter dropped, when there are any. They are asked once (`includeWeak`), marked "Less relevant" and ordered by relevance like all the others; a line at the end says they are shown and "Hide" takes them away again without a new search. It works with or without the synonyms.

**Memory.** The synonyms already asked are remembered ([synonymCache.js](../../lib/synonymCache.js.md)): the same search with the same model costs no new AI call, also in the next sessions (30 days). After the first visit the Home stays mounted, only hidden, when a document or a project is opened from it (`homeSeen` in `App.jsx`): coming back, the search, its results (also the less relevant ones shown), the synonyms found and the position in the list are as they were, with no new search. The refresh button reloads the network and repeats the current search.

The network is read again, silently, when the Home becomes visible again and every 5 seconds while the index is still working (`busy`: scan or vectors still to compute), so a project just added connects by itself when its vectors are ready; the network is not recomposed if nothing changed.

**Memory across project changes.** Opening a result in another project switches project, and during the switch `App.jsx` shows the loading screen instead of the whole interface, so the Home is unmounted as well (staying mounted only helps while the project does not change). The search therefore lives outside the component (`memory` in the module): text, results, synonyms found, "less relevant" shown and the scroll position of the list. Coming back, the Home restores them and does not repeat a search already done (`memory.done`: text and synonyms setting), nor call the AI again. The refresh button, or a new text, makes a new search. It lasts while the app stays open.

**Which projects the Home searches.** All the projects with the search index turned on, also the ones that do not share their knowledge: sharing decides only what the OTHER projects and their assistants see, the passages are in the index anyway and the Home search is the user's own. A result of a project that does not share carries a "Not shared" tag. The network keeps connecting only the projects that share (a project that does not share floats free).

The "Show less relevant too" action is styled as a link (accent colour, underlined) so it is clear that it can be clicked. When nothing is found the message is just "No relevant results".

**Project menu.** Right click on a bubble of the graph opens the same kind of menu as the projects list: Open (if it is not the open project), Project settings…, Move…, Show in the file manager. The settings open in the same window as in the list ([ProjectSettingsDialog.jsx](ProjectSettingsDialog.jsx.md)), drawn outside the dark graph area so it follows the app theme; after a change or a move the graph is reloaded. [App.jsx](../../App.jsx.md) passes `onMove`, `onOpenFile` and `onSettingsChanged`.

**Synonyms.** With the synonyms switch on, the first search already includes the synonyms found in the user's own texts (shown as "Also searched in your texts"). If the AI is on, its request starts at the same time as the first search, whatever the local synonyms are, and its terms are added in a second search ("Also searched"). With the AI off only the local ones are used, and the message says so.

The Refresh button reloads the graph and asks the main process to rescan the files of every project with the index on, opened or not (`getProjectGraph({ refresh: true })`); the graph keeps updating while the index works.

`projectsVersion` (from [App.jsx](../../App.jsx.md)) changes when the projects list changes next to the Home; the Home then reloads the graph, so a project removed from the list disappears from the graph without reopening the Home.

**Excerpts.** The excerpt of each passage is read as Markdown ([snippetMarkdown.js](../../lib/snippetMarkdown.js.md)): no formatting symbols, bold, italic, strikethrough and inline code in style, table rows as cells, and the found words highlighted.

**Workspaces.** The Home has no selector of its own for the active workspace (the one in the projects list is enough). The graph and the search cover the projects of the active workspace; the context menu of a bubble adds "Remove from workspace" inside a workspace, and "Add to the workspace" and "Ignore" for a temporary project (its tooltip also says "Temporary").

**Where the search looks.** The search bar is an input group: first the same selector as the projects list, in its scope mode ("All projects" and the workspaces, [WorkspaceSelector.jsx](WorkspaceSelector.jsx.md)), then the field. It starts on the active workspace (and follows it when it changes); it is absent when there are no workspaces. Choosing another entry changes only what that search covers, not the active workspace, and runs the search again. The choice goes to `projects:search` as `workspace` (`all` or an id); the results can include projects that are not in the graph on show. A workspace deleted while chosen sends the select back to the active one.

**Note at the bottom.** A short note at the bottom right appears only when there is something to know: the connections are approximate (no meaning model), there is not enough indexed text yet, or no project shares its knowledge. In the normal case (lines joining the projects closest in meaning) there is no note.
