# Projects Home

The network of all the projects and the search that works like an agent.

[Back to the technical documentation](../README.md) · [Back to the README](../../README.md)

The Projects tab opens the Home, a full-area view of all your projects that stays open even when the side panel is collapsed.

- **A network of projects.** Each project is a bubble with its icon (animated GIF and WebP icons move), sized and shaded by how much it contains. Projects whose contents are close in meaning are joined by synapses; the others float freely. A project inside the folder of another one is a smaller satellite orbiting its parent, even when it was never opened as a project of its own. Names show on hover, a right click opens the project menu (open, settings, move, show in the file manager), and you can drag bubbles, pan, and zoom. The graph has its own fixed dark background, whatever the theme.
- **Search like an agent.** The search bar looks through every project at once and shows the passages in a resizable panel, grouped by project, while the bubbles with results light up, brighter the more relevant the result, in the colours of a traffic light (green, amber, red) like the relevance in the list; a sub-project that holds the result lights up too. The words you searched are highlighted in the passages. Each result says why it is there (it contains the words, only matches by meaning, or both) and carries a relevance factor; passages that do not stand out from the rest are filtered, and "Show less relevant too" brings them back, always ordered by relevance. Opening a result opens its project and document, and the search and results are kept when you come back.
- **Synonyms.** Besides the words you typed, the search also uses the words of your own texts that are closest in meaning (computed locally from the vocabulary of your projects, no model call), and, if the AI is on, the synonyms an AI model suggests (the research model when one is set, which only sees the searched words), cached for recent searches. A switch turns the synonyms off.
- **Whole words.** A button turns the search into an exact one: the whole question is looked for as a phrase, whole words in the order you wrote them, so there are no synonyms and no search by meaning. It is the way to find a name, an expression or a quotation as it stands.
- **Several windows.** The arrangement of the bubbles is shared by all the windows of the application: when you move bubbles in one window, the others slide into the new arrangement at once.
- **Always up to date.** The Refresh button rescans the files of every project with the index on, opened or not, and the projects are also updated in the background when you open one.
